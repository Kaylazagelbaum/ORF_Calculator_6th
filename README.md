  As a sixth grade teacher, one of my responsibilities was assessing my students' progress in fundemental skills, such as reading fluency and reading comprehension. My school, Be'er Hagolah Institute, used the Acadience Oral Reading Fluency model to do so. After administering the beginning of the year benchmark assessment and spending hours calculating composite scores and benchmark rankings, I decided to build a website that would do the tedious work for me. This program cut down the time it took to analyze the results in subsequent assessments by over 50% and helped clearly illustrate, through the color coded blocks, where a students' cabalities are holding.
