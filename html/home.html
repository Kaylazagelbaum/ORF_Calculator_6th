Acadience Oral Fluency

Welcome to the ORF assessment manager! You can use this site to access assessment material, calculate composite reading scores, and recognize where the cut points for risk are in any given category.

What is the ORF?

Acadience Oral Reading Fluency (ORF) is a universal assessment system that provides accurate evaluation and progress monitoring to ensure students receive targeted support. The assessments are thoroughly researched, reliable, and valid, as well as efficient and economical. The results of the Acadience assessment will reveal which students need additional support, and specifically in which areas, by measuring individual scores in comparison to grade-appropriate benchmarks.

How does it work?

There are two segments of the assessment:

ORF - Students will have one minute to read a passage. The teacher will record the total words read and the percent that was read correctly. Then, the student will have one minute to tell the teacher, in his own words, what he read about. The teacher will record the number of words in the recall and the quality of the summary.

MAZE - Students will read a passage with words missing from sentences. For each missing word, there will be a box with three options. The student will circle the word that fits best within the context of the passage. The teacher will record an adjusted score that considers total words completed and percent accuracy.
